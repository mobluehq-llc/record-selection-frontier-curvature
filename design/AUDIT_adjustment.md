# Audit: why mb_sota size-adjusted power RISES for the early logistic (4.8% raw -> 26% adjusted)  [2026-11-23]

[internal operations note redacted]

**Verdict: not a bug in the statistic, the bootstrap or the cutoff arithmetic. A tail-rule mismatch between the raw test and the adjustment code in `analyze_design.py`.**
1. mb_sota (and mb_all, hb_*, xb_*) is a percentile-CI test: reject iff the 95% CI of the quadratic coefficient excludes 0. With z = (lo+hi)/(hi-lo) that is exactly |z| > 1 (verified on all 3000 null replicates, all six CI tests: `True`).
2. `analyze_design.zstat` + `main()` define "size-adjusted two-sided power" as z > q97.5(null z) or z < q2.5(null z): equal tails of 2.5%, NOT |z|>1.
3. Under the exponential null the SOTA bootstrap distribution is biased upward (selection of records bends the SOTA trajectory): null z for mb_sota has quantiles 2.5/50/97.5 = -0.643 / +0.165 / +1.124. So the raw "5.2% two-sided" size is **5.10% upward (z>1) + 0.07% downward (z<-1)**, i.e. effectively a one-sided 5% test for acceleration that essentially cannot reject for deceleration.
4. The adjusted rule sets the lower cutoff at -0.643 (not -1) and the upper at +1.124 (not +1). Lowering the lower cutoff from -1 to -0.643 lets many more early-logistic replicates count as rejections (4.8% -> 26.3% downward); raising the upper cutoff from 1 to 1.124 lowers the upward power (rr=2: 19.2% -> 12.7%). The two rules are different two-sided rules on the same statistic, so the rejection sets are not nested. The referee's "nested" argument holds for ONE common symmetric-or-same cutoff; here the cutoffs are different on each side.

**Which earlier numbers change (DESIGN_RESULTS.md Q2 table, `summary_base`):** every "adj" entry of a percentile-CI test whose null z is asymmetric: mb_sota (large shift), hb_sota (null z quantiles -0.90 / +0.70 / +2.30: raw null 36.5% up / 1.2% down), hb_all, xb_sota, and to a lesser degree xb_all and mb_all. The adjusted numbers were comparisons at "2.5% per tail", not at the raw rule's size; read as signed one-sided 5% power they differ. Examples (A_sup_rr2, upward): mb_sota raw 19.2%, adjusted 12.7% (2.5% tail); at a matched 5.1% upward size the adjusted power is 19.2% by construction. The raw rates, their CP intervals, the size tables and the iid_*/pb_sota rows (p-value based, same two-sided rule both ways) are unaffected. Not affected: the conclusion that mb_sota is blind to deceleration at its raw rule (raw 4.8% downward at early logistic; the 26% is power of a different rule at a 2.5% lower tail).

**Remedy (Design Amendment 2):** tails are reported separately (upward 5%, downward 5%, two-sided 2.5+2.5), adjusted power is defined per tail with the cutoff taken from the independent calibration null, achieved size is validated on separate null draws, and mean estimated curvature is reported so that an asymmetric null is visible. `analyze_design.py` itself is left unchanged (it is the record of what was computed).
